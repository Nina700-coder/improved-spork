Scientific Transparency: How Step Calculations Work

Individual Stride Length Matters: A human stride length is directly influenced by height, walking cadence, and terrain incline (typically varying between 0.65m and 0.85m). For this reason, distance and step predictions in Lucky Step are reliable mathematical estimates calibrated to an average stride length of 0.76 meters. You can fine-tune your custom stride length in our planner below for enhanced accuracy!

Evidence-Based Wellness

The Proven Science of Regular Walking

Walking is one of the most accessible, low-impact, and scientifically validated forms of preventive healthcare. Here is how daily purposeful steps elevate your longevity and mental performance.

🫀 -25% to -30%

Cardiovascular Resilience

Regular walking promotes arterial elasticity, lowers resting blood pressure, and significantly reduces long-term coronary risk through steady, sustained Zone 2 circulation.

🧠 +35% BDNF

Cognitive Clarity & Neurogenesis

Physical motion stimulates Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF), enhancing memory consolidation, creative ideation, and mental resilience during stressful days.

☀️ Circadian Reset

Natural Circadian Alignment

Walking outdoors exposes your retinal ganglion cells to natural daylight, naturally timing cortisol release in the morning and promoting deep, restorative sleep at night.

⚡ NEAT Boost

Metabolic Longevity & NEAT

Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT) accounts for the largest fraction of daily metabolic expenditure, supporting glucose regulation without the high cortisol spikes of extreme workouts.
